Privacy Policy

LANtern – Network Scanner · Last updated: 2026

In short: LANtern collects no personal data, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. All analysis of your network happens entirely on your device. No scan results are sent to us or to third parties.

1. Controller

The party responsible for this app under the GDPR is the developer of LANtern. Contact for privacy enquiries: lemmidevpixelcraft@gmail.com.

2. What data does the app process?

LANtern is a local tool for analysing your own Wi-Fi or LAN network. The app processes the following, exclusively on your device:

  • IP addresses, MAC addresses (where provided by the system), hostnames, vendor identifiers and open ports of the devices on your own network;
  • Wi-Fi information about your currently connected network (e.g. the network name), where iOS makes it available;
  • device names and notes you assign, and the scans or signal patterns you save.

All of this data stays exclusively local on your device (in the app's database). It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

3. No data collection by the developer

LANtern contains no analytics, tracking or advertising libraries. No usage statistics, identifiers or personal data are sent to us. There are no user accounts.

4. Outbound network connections

The only connection LANtern makes beyond your local network is a request to Apple's public endpoint (itunes.apple.com) to check whether a newer version of the app is available. No personal data is transmitted in this request. The built-in browser only connects to devices you tap yourself.

5. Permissions

  • Local Network: required to find devices on your network — the core purpose of the app.
  • Location (While Using): iOS only reveals Wi-Fi details such as the network name if location permission is granted. Your location is neither stored nor transmitted by the app.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you enable alerts for new devices.

6. Purchases

Optional Pro features are handled through Apple's in-app purchase. Payment is processed by Apple; we receive no payment or credit card data. Apple's Privacy Policy also applies.

7. Children

LANtern is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

8. Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, we hold nothing for you to access, correct or delete. You can remove the data stored locally in the app at any time by deleting individual entries or uninstalling the app.

9. Legal note on use

LANtern may only be used on networks you own or have explicit permission to scan. Scanning networks without authorisation may be a criminal offence (in Germany e.g. § 202a StGB, in Austria § 118a StGB). You alone are responsible for lawful use.

10. Changes

I may update this policy as the app evolves. The current version is always available on this page.
